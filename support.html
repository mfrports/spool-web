Turning Spool on in Safari

Open Spool once, then in Safari choose Settings › Extensions, tick Spool and allow it on every website. Its record appears in Safari's toolbar. Spool's own window says when the extension is still off.

Saving a video

Play the video, then click the record in Safari's toolbar. Spool lists what the page plays, with its qualities and sizes. Click Download, or choose a quality first. Or copy the video's address and press Command V in Spool's window.

Where the videos go

Into your Downloads folder, unless you choose another one at the foot of Spool's window. Drag a folder onto it to use that one. Finished videos are MP4 wherever the picture and sound allow, so QuickTime and the Finder play them.

Spool finds nothing on a page

  • Start the video first: many sites only fetch it once it plays.
  • If Spool was turned on after the page opened, reload the page.
  • Some players never show a stream. Spool then offers to record the player: it plays the video quickly and keeps what comes.

"Copy protected"

Video protected with DRM, as on subscription streaming services, cannot be saved. Spool shows it as protected and leaves it alone.

Live streams

A live stream is recorded from when you start until you press stop. What came is kept and joined into one file.

A download stopped with an error

Links to streams often expire. Play the video again on its page and press Try again in Spool.
