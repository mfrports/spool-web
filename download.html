Every video.

Everything the App Store's copy does, and YouTube, Vimeo and some 1800 more sites besides. yt-dlp comes inside and keeps itself current.

Download Spool for Mac Free. macOS 26 or later, on a Mac with Apple silicon.

Some 1800 sites

YouTube, Vimeo, Twitch, the public broadcasters: Spool reads each the way its player does, picks the best picture QuickTime plays, and keeps the original sound.

Nothing to install

yt-dlp, the Python it runs on and FFmpeg come inside Spool. No Homebrew, no Terminal, nothing else on your Mac is touched.

Always current

Sites change their players every few weeks. Spool fetches each new yt-dlp from its makers, checks it and tries it before using it, and updates itself too.

Notarized by Apple

Signed and checked by Apple like every app from the App Store, so it opens with a double click.

Getting started

  1. Open Spool.dmg and drag Spool into Applications.
  2. Open Spool once. Its window says what to do next.
  3. In Safari choose Settings › Extensions, tick Spool and allow it on every website.

Have Spool from the App Store already? Keep one of the two: to Safari they are the same app, and this one does more.

Video protected with DRM stays protected: Spool never decrypts anything. Save only what you have the right to keep.
